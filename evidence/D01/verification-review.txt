Strongest reasonable objection: is an external teacher write to a past-day tab
during archival actually supported?
- Objection: SETUP.md:108 says queue writes at night are impossible, the
  backend closes lazily; the ADR's teacher editing may mean the current queue
  tab, not a past-day tab being archived. The test injects the write inside a
  fake batch_update, which is not a real Google operation.
- Answer: the support is explicit in this PR's own documentation, not assumed.
  SETUP.md:97-105 states the contract for both closers, and :129-130 explicitly
  names the changed-during-close case and promises the tab will be kept.
  Code.gs:165-168 implements exactly that guard, so the backend is the deviating
  side. ADR:110-113 makes direct teacher editing a stated feature.
- The test does not rely on an impossible partial batch: the hook runs before
  the fake applies any request, which models a write landing after the backend's
  read and before the batch starts. Google's batch being atomic does not remove
  that window; the missing re-read/compare is the defect. A silent partial
  appendCells is explicitly NOT claimed as a separate defect.
- The short window and the fact that production timing was not measured are
  real limits of evidence, but they do not refute the documented contract.

Limits: in-memory fake, no real Sheets/Apps Script/network; no restart timing
test; multi-worker is not claimed.
